8. BACKLOAD DEALS ENGINE (SHARED-LOAD RETURNING VEHICLES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Suggestion:
Build a public dashboard displaying "Upcoming Return Trips" or "Backload Deals". When your closed container truck shifts goods from e.g. Ranchi to Delhi, it usually returns empty. The system will display: "Delhi to Ranchi Container returning empty on 22nd July — Book remaining space at 60% off."

Reasoning:
Empty return trips ("dry runs") are the single biggest profit killer in long-distance trucking. Since the primary customer has already paid for the round-trip distance, booking cargo on the return trip generates pure, 100% margin profit. Offering these discounted rates captures budget-conscious consumers who would otherwise book low-cost local operators.

Pros:
- Converts empty truck return legs into highly profitable shared-load runs.
- Offers highly competitive prices to capture the lower-tier market without diluting brand margins.
- Increases truck utilization efficiency.

Cons:
- Requires active dashboard management by coordinators to log upcoming truck return schedules.
- Requires flexibility in customer dispatch dates to match vehicle schedules.
