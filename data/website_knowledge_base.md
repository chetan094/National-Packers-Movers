## 2. Core Shifting Services & Operational Workflows
1. **Household Relocation (Ghar ka Saman):**
   * *Packaging:* Multi-layer heavy-duty packing using premium bubble wrap, corrugated sheets, waterproof stretch wrap, and edge-guards for electronics and furniture.
   * *Fragiles:* Separate carton boxes with internal foam sheet layering and bubble padding for kitchenware, glassware, and decorative items.
   * *Transit:* Enclosed container trucks protect against rain, dust, and pilferage during transit.
2. **Corporate & Office Relocation:**
   * *Execution:* Timed weekend moves to ensure zero operational business downtime.
   * *Assets Packing:* Server racks, computers, and files packed in shockproof plastic crates.
   * *Tagging:* Color-coded box inventory tags mapped for accurate desk-to-desk setup at the destination.
3. **Industrial Shifting:**
   * *Machinery:* Heavy plant equipment, generators, and industrial materials.
   * *Equipment:* Handled using cranes, forklifts, hydraulic jacks, and flatbed trailers.
   * *Safety:* Supervised by senior site managers utilizing heavy cargo lashing configurations.
4. **Vehicle Transportation:**
   * *Cars:* Transported via specialized double-deck enclosed car carriers (scratch-free delivery guarantee).
   * *Bikes:* Wrapped in double-layer foam padding with scratch checklists.
   * *Inspection:* Pre-loading tyre, fuel, and scratch reports filled and signed by client.

## 6. PSU & Corporate Reimbursement Guidelines
Most government bodies, railways, public banks (like SBI), and PSUs (like Coal India, SAIL, NTPC, DVC) require a standardized set of documentation to process relocation allowance claims. We provide a 100% genuine, HR-audit approved document set aligning with these strict guidelines:

### Standard Relocation Claim Document Set:
1. **GST Tax Invoice / Bill:**
   * Itemized breakdown of charges: Packing, Loading, Carriage, Unloading, Unpacking, and Insurance.
   * Registered GSTIN: `20AIHPJ7005R1Z6`.
   * Standard HSN Code: 9965 (Goods Transport Services).
2. **Consignment Note / Lorry Receipt (LR):**
   * Records the specific carrier truck number (e.g. `JH-10-CD-XXXX`).
   * Lists origin and destination addresses, consigner, and consignee details.
3. **Itemized Packing List (Checksheet):**
   * Detailed checklist inventory count of all boxes, furniture, and appliances with condition stamps.
4. **Stamped Money Receipt:**
   * Official proof of payment showing the amount, date, and cashier stamp.

### GST Rate Classifications:
* **5% GST (GTA Scheme):** Lower tax rate, suitable for claims where input tax credit is not claimed.
* **18% GST (Packers & Movers):** Standard rate for full-service shifting (material, labor, transport combined).
